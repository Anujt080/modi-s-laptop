# THE ABANDONED COMPUTER: POLITICAL ARCHIVE EDITION
### Gandhi Hackathon — OpenLake × Meraz × IIT Bhilai

> *"In November 2026, a decommissioned Windows 98 terminal was discovered in a basement crate in the Gandhinagar Secretariat (Sachivalaya), Gujarat. Last active in November 2016, the partially corrupted workstation belonged to R.K. Dave, a fictional administrative employee associated with the Gujarat Chief Minister's Office. Booting the computer uncovers an interactive, historically rigorous political archive spanning October 2001 to November 2016."*

---

## 🏛️ Central Storyline & Historical Archive (2001–2016)

The original workstation (`GNR-CMO-ADM-98`) operated in Gandhinagar, Gujarat, and was decommissioned during office relocations immediately following the **8 November 2016 Demonetisation announcement**.

### Strict Timeline Rule
* **Cutoff Date**: November 2016.
* Documents, emails, photographs, and web browsing history strictly conclude in November 2016.
* Demonetisation (8 November 2016) is the final major recorded event; no post-2016 developments appear on the original hard drives.

   * Inspecting the Mahatma Mandir photograph under the UV scanner reveals a hidden watermark: `PASSWORD: SACHIVALAYA`.
2. **Encrypted Dossier (`C:\SECRET\confidential_transfer_order.doc`)**:
   * Password protected. Entering `SACHIVALAYA` unlocks the top-secret decommissioning directive.
3. **Sector Recovery Protocol (`MS-DOS Prompt`)**:
   * Typing `recover` reconstructs the corrupted cluster `0x8F91` into `recovered_archivist_confession.txt`.
4. **Historical Milestones (`MS-DOS Prompt`)**:
   * Type `timeline` to stream chronological political milestones from 2001 to November 2016.
   * Type `archive` for an indexing report of all dossiers.

   * Read R.K. Dave's account of saving duplicate copies of the archive before leaving in November 2016.

## 🔌 Final Shutdown Experience

Clicking **Start -> Shut Down...** prompts the classic Windows 98 dialog.
Selecting **Shut down** displays the memorable closing sequence:
1. *"System archive successfully recovered."*
2. *"Last recorded activity: November 2016."*
3. *"Some files were never recovered."*
4. Transitions to the iconic screen: **"It's now safe to turn off your computer."**
5. Interactive buttons allow users to reboot back into the 2026 discovery or resume exploring.
